01 Overview & Scope

Reclav Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Reclav", "we", "our", or "us") operates an enterprise AI-powered hospital revenue intelligence platform. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, disclose, and safeguard information when hospital clients and their authorized personnel access our platform.

This policy applies to all data processed through our platform, our website at reclav.com, and any related services. By using our services, you agree to the practices described herein.

02 Data We Collect

As a B2B enterprise platform, we process two distinct categories of data:

  • Client Account Data: Business contact details, names, email addresses, and billing information of hospital administrators and authorized users who manage accounts.
  • Operational Data: Data provided by hospital clients to power our AI agents — including anonymized or pseudonymized billing records, insurance claim histories, OPD scheduling data, and operational metrics. We process this strictly as instructed by our hospital clients.
  • Platform Usage Data: Logs of how users interact with our command center dashboard, for the purpose of improving system performance and security.
  • Waitlist Data: Email addresses and hospital names submitted via our early-access waitlist, used solely to communicate about pilot access.
We do not collect, store, or process individual patient personally identifiable information (PII) directly. All clinical data processed by our agents is handled in pseudonymized or de-identified form under the data governance framework of our hospital clients.
03 DPDP Act Compliance

Reclav is designed from the ground up to comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India. Our architecture respects the distinction between Data Fiduciaries (our hospital clients) and Data Processors (Reclav).

  • We act as a Data Processor for all patient-adjacent data provided by hospital clients.
  • Processing is conducted strictly under a written data processing agreement with each client.
  • We maintain Records of Processing Activities (RoPA) in compliance with applicable regulations.
  • We support our hospital clients in fulfilling their obligations as Data Fiduciaries under the DPDP Act.
04 How We Use the Data

We use the data we process exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, maintain, and improve our AI revenue intelligence agents.
  • To identify revenue leakage patterns and trigger automated recovery workflows on behalf of clients.
  • To improve the accuracy and performance of our machine learning models, using only aggregated and de-identified signals — never individual patient records.
  • To generate reports and dashboards within the Reclav Command Center for authorized hospital personnel.
  • To communicate with waitlist registrants about platform availability and pilot program updates.
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.
05 Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted infrastructure partners (cloud hosting, monitoring) who are contractually bound to protect data and cannot use it for independent purposes.
  • Legal Requirements: Where disclosure is required by Indian law, a court order, or a lawful government authority.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger or acquisition, data may be transferred subject to equivalent privacy protections.

All third-party service providers are vetted for security compliance and operate under data processing agreements consistent with DPDP Act requirements.

06 Data Security

Security is foundational to Reclav's architecture, not an afterthought. We employ:

  • AES-256 encryption for all data at rest and TLS 1.3 for all data in transit.
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) ensuring staff can only access data necessary for their function.
  • Secure, audited integration protocols for HIS and TPA portal connections.
  • Continuous human-in-the-loop monitoring for anomalous access patterns.
  • Regular penetration testing and third-party security audits.
In the event of a data security incident, we will notify affected hospital clients within 72 hours of becoming aware, in accordance with applicable notification requirements under the DPDP Act.
07 Data Retention

We retain Processed Data only for as long as necessary to provide services under the active client agreement, or as required by applicable Indian law. Our standard retention schedule is:

  • Active client operational data: Retained for the duration of the contract plus 12 months for audit purposes.
  • Waitlist contact data: Retained until the recipient opts out or the program concludes.
  • Platform usage logs: Retained for 90 days for security and performance monitoring.

Upon contract termination, all client operational data is securely deleted or returned in a portable format within 30 days of the client's written request.

08 Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act and applicable law, authorized representatives of our hospital clients have the right to:

  • Access information about what data we hold on behalf of their organization.
  • Request correction of inaccurate client account information.
  • Request deletion of account data upon contract termination.
  • Receive a portable copy of data in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent for processing of non-essential data communications.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer using the details below.

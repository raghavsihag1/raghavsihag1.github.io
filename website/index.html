Root Causes

Why This Problem Has Proven So Hard to Solve

The causes are structural, not superficial. Hiring more billing staff or replacing your HIS won't fix them.

01

Data is Everywhere, Visible Nowhere

Patient records, billing, insurance status, and clinical notes exist in isolated systems that never talk to each other in real time. No one in your hospital has a live, complete view of what is happening to revenue at any given moment.

02

Reactive, Not Preventive

By the time a claim is denied, a slot is empty, or a patient disputes a bill — the damage is done. Every revenue recovery effort is remediation. The systems simply do not exist to intervene before the loss occurs.

03

Insurance Ecosystem Complexity

With 30+ TPAs each operating different pre-auth workflows, documentation requirements, and coding standards — the only way to stay consistently compliant is with automated, insurer-specific intelligence.

04

Volume Outgrows Manual Capacity

A 150-bed hospital running at capacity generates hundreds of chargeable events, insurance interactions, and billing touchpoints every single day. Human teams cannot monitor all of it without structural gaps.

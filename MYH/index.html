Our Founder

Rekha Kumari

Dynamic Youth Leader, Social Activist & MUN Organiser

7 Years of MUN Excellence

Rekha Kumari is a dynamic youth leader and social activist heavily involved in civic engagement and leadership development across Chandigarh, Delhi, Goa, Hyderabad and Punjab. With 7 years of MUN experience, she has been part of renowned organisations including The National Youth Parliament (Goa), Uthan Youth Parliament (Delhi), Unnati, DMUN, Muskurahat Foundation, Chandigarh Youth Circuit, and the National Youth Parliament.

Civic Leadership & Advocacy

She plays a vital role in organising simulated legislative assemblies that train young minds in public policy, diplomacy, and political debate. As a UNICEF U-Report Voice of Youth advocate, she champions youth-led solutions to regional and national issues.

Writer · Youth Ki Awaaz

She shares her perspectives on social and environmental matters as a writer for Youth Ki Awaaz, solidifying her reputation as an impactful advocate for youth empowerment and active citizenship.
